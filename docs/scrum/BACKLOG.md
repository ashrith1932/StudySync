# StudySync Master Product Backlog

**Prioritization Method:** MoSCoW (Must Have, Should Have, Could Have, Won't Have)  
**Estimation Scale:** Fibonacci Story Points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13)  

---

## Product Backlog Items (PBI)

| PBI ID | Priority | User Story | Story Points | Target Increment | Target Sprint | DoD Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **US-01** | Must Have | As a student, I want to register and log in securely, so that I can access personalized group features. | 5 | Increment 1 | Sprint 1 | Planned |
| **US-02** | Must Have | As a student, I want to configure my major, learning goals, and weekly availability slots, so that matching recommendations reflect my schedule. | 5 | Increment 1 | Sprint 1 | Planned |
| **US-03** | Must Have | As a student, I want to create a new study group with name, subject, and member cap, so that I can initiate collaborative learning. | 5 | Increment 1 | Sprint 1 | Planned |
| **US-04** | Must Have | As a student, I want to search and filter study groups by subject and tags, so that I can locate groups relevant to my courses. | 5 | Increment 1 | Sprint 1 | Planned |
| **US-05** | Must Have | As a student, I want to apply to join a study group with an introductory note, so that the group owner can evaluate my request. | 3 | Increment 2 | Sprint 2 | Planned |
| **US-06** | Must Have | As a group owner, I want to view, accept, or reject pending membership requests, so that I can manage group composition and capacity. | 5 | Increment 2 | Sprint 2 | Planned |
| **US-07** | Should Have | As a student, I want to view recommended study groups ranked by a compatibility score, so that I find peers with overlapping courses and availability. | 8 | Increment 2 | Sprint 2 | Planned |
| **US-08** | Must Have | As a group owner, I want to schedule study sessions with dates, times, and meeting locations, so that group members can plan their study. | 5 | Increment 3 | Sprint 3 | Planned |
| **US-09** | Should Have | As a system, I want to validate study session time boundaries and detect scheduling conflicts, so that students do not double-book sessions. | 5 | Increment 3 | Sprint 3 | Planned |
| **US-10** | Must Have | As a student, I want to view upcoming study sessions on a calendar view, so that I can organize my weekly academic schedule. | 5 | Increment 3 | Sprint 3 | Planned |
| **US-11** | Should Have | As an enrolled member, I want to RSVP (`attending`/`declined`) and check into active sessions, so that my attendance record is tracked. | 3 | Increment 3 | Sprint 3 | Planned |
| **US-12** | Should Have | As a group member, I want to share study resources (links, files, notes), so that my group has centralized study materials. | 5 | Increment 3 | Sprint 3 | Planned |
| **US-13** | Must Have | As a team, we want to perform complete end-to-end regression testing and security checks, so that the platform is robust for release. | 8 | Increment 4 | Sprint 4 | Planned |
| **US-14** | Could Have | As a student, I want dark-mode UI support, so that I can study comfortably at night. | 2 | Increment 4 | Sprint 4 | Planned |
| **US-15** | Won't Have | As a student, I want integrated video calls directly in the browser. (Deferred to v2; external meeting links used). | - | Post-v1 | - | Excluded |
